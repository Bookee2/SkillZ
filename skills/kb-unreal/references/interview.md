# Interview: rounds and question bank

Use AskUserQuestion, up to 4 questions a round, the recommended option first
with "(Recommended)". Pre-fill options from research (the brand, the sets that
fit, the proven blocks). Show concepts in round 1 or 2. Stop when answers stop
changing the plan. Typical rounds:

## 1. Purpose and audience
- What is it for? (channel intro/outro, logo reveal, launch film, commercial,
  product hero, flyover, data story, social cut, event loop)
- Who sees it, and where does it play? (YouTube 16:9, Shorts/Reels 9:16,
  website hero loop, LED screen, pitch deck)
- Length and deadline. (Idents 10–15 s; end screens 20 s; commercials 30–44 s;
  hero loops 8–20 s seamless)
- Is it part of a set? (intro + end screen + vertical cut; the intro must end
  on the wordmark and the outro start on it)

## 2. Story and feel
- The beat: what's on screen before the brand, what's the turn, what's the
  reveal, how long the hold is.
- Feel: cinematic / playful / premium / technical / raw; reference films or
  idents he likes or hates.
- Must-haves: logo, product, character, real data, a place.
- Pitch the concepts here (real names, one line each, proven vs new, risk).

## 3. World and material
- Which set: Motion Design stage, Saturn platform, canyon/forest, caves, open
  world biome, city street, real terrain, abstract particle void.
- What assets exist: Meshy models (will Kris make new ones? which pose,
  which clips), logo files, product models/CAD, footage, data feeds.
- Brand: palette and which colour leads; lettering (Kris's own vs a font);
  what's off-limits.
- Text on screen: none / wordmark only / taglines / CTA / captions.

## 4. Sound, formats and delivery
- Sound: Mixkit library mix (which palette), synthesised, Kris's own music
  (deliver silent + SFX), or silent.
- Formats: aspect ratios, resolution (draft 720p; final 4K for YouTube),
  fps (30 for idents, 24 for film looks), alpha needed, loop needed.
- Variants: A/B sound mixes, day/night, short/long cuts, vertical crops.
- Delivery: where files go (repo output, iCloud, client drive), naming.

## 5. Constraints and process
- Budget/time; how many concept drafts to see.
- Mac limits that matter (no nDisplay, Niagara Fluids beyond Gas 3D, untiled
  path tracing); OK to need a Windows machine?
- Licensing for client work (Fab Standard: rendered video fine; no raw
  assets).
- Should it be reusable (a template for future clients, Editor Utility
  buttons, a Remote Control panel)?
- Will this become a making-of video (capture prompts, keep a version log)?

## Questions learned from past misses (ask these early)
- "Should the camera end dead centre at eye level?" (letters ended tilted)
- "How much glow — should the colours read clearly?" (glow 2.3 too bright)
- "Should the explosion/transition be the hero moment? Colour or white heat?"
- "Does the opening need to establish the place (e.g. the whole planet)?"
- "Bigger wordmark, smaller tagline? Stacked or one line?" (Capra lockup)
- "Is there a sound library you'd rather use than synthesis?"
- "What should the end screen keep clear for YouTube elements?"
