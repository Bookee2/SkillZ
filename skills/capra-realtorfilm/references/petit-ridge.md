## The site
A steep 4.9-acre lot (709–793 m, faces south-east) 1.9 km from and 250 m above Lake Petit. Concept cabin (a CC BY
Sketchfab stone cabin, inches → metres) on a 25 × 17 m cut-and-fill pad, deck to bearing 135, 25 m inside the
lines; a 45 m building clearing and a selectively cleared view corridor (120 m deep, ±35°). Lake view from the deck:
82 % bare earth, 32 % with an assumed 20 m canopy, **20 % (21 of 105 acres) with the real lidar canopy**.
