## What broke (each is now fixed in landfilm/ and in the lessons)
- The elevation service answers 502 for 4033 px: 2017 px blocks. Overpass: 406 without a User-Agent, 502 when busy.
- One lidar project picked by name (Rutherford for a Buncombe lot) → all overlapping projects, prefixed files;
  NC county flights are ~50 points/m² → stop at the octree depth that reaches ~6/m².
- Approach copied too far out (2.6 km): 188 m/s at 12 s → Petit Ridge's distances.
- Petit Ridge's Z0 and elevation range baked into the terrain shader → Read the Land at the wrong heights.
- Lawns and pasture read as sand → open-ground mask with the photo's blurred colour; a Grayscale sampler then broke
  the material silently (Default Material); then lime lawns and a lemon clearing → luminance caps.
- Card honesty: a big reservoir's "5 % of the water" → acres; "long-range" only past 8 km median; "more than 40 km"
  at the model's edge; the shoreline from the listing when it disagrees with the county lines.
- Not fixed: the lot line under a closed canopy is mostly hidden (Little Potts); the 54 s swing past the house is
  quick; narrow creeks don't render (no water mesh under 30 m wide).
